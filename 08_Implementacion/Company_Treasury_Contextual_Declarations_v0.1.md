# Company Treasury Observations and Contextual Declarations v0.1

New reviews v12 can conserve explicit TreasuryObservation entries (coherent, inconsistent or not established), then TreasuryContextualDeclaration entries for source correspondence, economic cutoff, amount support, availability, restrictions and source sufficiency. The service reuses FIN-TREASURY-SUPPORT-01 and FIN-TREASURY-CONTEXT-01 public builders without modifying core contracts.

Observations require a responsible person and note. Declared consistency requires conserved documentary support. Omitted observations remain pending; a NOT_ESTABLISHED or inconsistent result is not confirmation. Corrections append a new support revision; the original declaration, bytes and prior observations remain conserved in history.

Contextual declarations explicitly supply applicability, necessity and their reasons, impact, support assessment and reason, criterion reference/version and optional links to actual recorded observations. A criterion must be conserved in the exact preparation. Declared sufficiency requires a locator in the conserved origin. No observation link or declaration is created implicitly. The first form uses treasury-support documents only, with no additional assessment material invented.

Public factories reproduce the exact preparation and support before either submission; stale anchors or revisions are rejected. Changing support marks the earlier assessment as previous material. A new assessment for different support starts without inheriting old declarations. Append-only company_treasury_contextual_assessment rows preserve canonical payloads/fingerprints and revisions.

These remain bound local declarations, not authenticated reviewer designation, Treasury QualityCheck output, evidence elevation, operational admission or purchase viability. Even six positive/sufficient declarations do not execute quality or change financial evidence. Existing projections and historical print remain unchanged. Current screen/print distinguish observations, contextual declarations and omissions; commercial drafts exclude internal results.

Tests cover exact bindings and criteria, positive-support requirements, negative outcomes, omitted conditions, explicit observation links, stale scope/revisions, history, changed-support reset, no promotion with all conditions declared, HTML escaping, frozen reporting, draft privacy and HTTP token checks. Full Python/JavaScript/SQL Server CI remains required. Visual QA remains pending because the cloud browser cannot reach the loopback-only development server.

Local validation: eight new contextual/observation tests, 16 existing support/report checks, and the full pytest suite (2,709 passed). Visual QA remains pending.
