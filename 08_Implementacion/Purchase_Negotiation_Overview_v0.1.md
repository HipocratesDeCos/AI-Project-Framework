# Purchase negotiation overview

The second visible purchase step groups the existing negotiation controls and the
read-only A–L stock explanation above a three-part state summary, dated scenario
milestones, and Money / Stock / Alternatives evidence cards.

## Boundaries

- All commercial, cash and stock calculations still use the existing endpoints.
- The stock digest mirrors the original planning explanation, including incomplete
  states. It creates no inputs, declarations or independent calculations.
- Each proposal retains its independent analysis, explicit payment selection and
  payment calendar. An analysis does not choose or accept conditions.
- The timeline displays actual returned dates. Unknown dates say Pending. It does
  not invent a shortage date, delivery confirmation or receipt-day balance.
- Inventory-date figures and order-date assumptions may legitimately differ. A
  contrast is informational; it is not an automatic discrepancy verdict.
- Additional charts, alternatives and evidence remain accessible in disclosures.
- Saved studies remain read-only and retain their existing rendering ownership.
- No changes to accounting, authority, financial activation or persistence rules.

## Verification

`purchase_negotiation_overview.cjs`: four phases, A–L, live source values,
invalidation, unique controls, timeline/evidence ownership, dark mobile layout.

`purchase_conditions_layout.cjs`: comparison geometry, evidence placement,
restoration across steps, saved-study ownership, return to draft, mobile overflow.

`purchase_option_studies.cjs`: independent results, explicit selection, independent
calendars, frozen saved reports, copy workflow and absence of acceptance promotion.
